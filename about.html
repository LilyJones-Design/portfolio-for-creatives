  • Howdy partner!

    My name is Lily Jones, and I’m a multidisciplinary artist who specializes in illustration, motion and web design. I have an intense passion for the traditional arts and tech, and exploring the connection between both is what really inspires me: I love science fiction books and listen to loads of electronic music; they’ve inspired my most recent work in design, exploring visual development and motion graphics. I'm also mildly obsessed with horses and the old west, if that wasn't obvious.

    Ultimately, I want to create work with substance that brings genuine value to people, addressing the questions and issues that they care about, and speak out for what’s right through the vehicle of genre.
